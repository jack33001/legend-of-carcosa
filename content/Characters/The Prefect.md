A large gorilla-like [[Porcelain Soldier]], with another [[Porcelain Soldier]] resembling a mage sitting atop. This creature controlled where [[The Leviathan]] would shoot during [[The Sunrise Seige]]. The construct was manufactured by [[The Lord of Puppets]] and given to [[The Red Quarter Rebels]].
