Master Lurat was the primary teacher of [[Hen]] Thornmeadow. This aging human had mastered the secrets of the Sevenfold Path. His once black hair had always been white to [[Hen]] who regarded the aging master with the deepest respect. Lurat was the final one to give up on [[Hen]] growing any larger once his halfling heritage showed itself amongst the human monastery. In the end Lurat embraced [[Hen]] as the final member of the Sevenfold Path and charged him with the killing of all monsters.
