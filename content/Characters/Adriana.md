[[Gino]]’s wife, whose untimely death marked the beginning of his disillusionment with the family. [[Adriana]] was fiercely loyal to [[Gino]] but wary of [[L’ombra Profonda (The Tartuffo Family)]], often urging him to envision a life beyond [[Portovino]]’s grasp. Her death in a mafia “accident” shook [[Gino]]’s faith, revealing the extent of his family’s ruthlessness and disregard for human life.
