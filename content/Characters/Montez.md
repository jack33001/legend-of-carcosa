Full name Montez Wildes, a sleazy sailor from [[Shai]] who approached [[The Blackstars]] in [[Bridgemoore]] and offered his services navigating [[The Maw]]. He showed up at [[The Second Sunset]] the morning after an encounter with (presumably) [[The Lord of the Hunt]] without us telling him our location.

While navigating [[The Maw]], he tosses a stone up and down; that stone slightly pulls in a direction, in which he tells [[Gino]] to steer. He has a pouch of stones, which he says he collects from all the places he goes - the one he tosses is from [[Carcosa]], near one of [[Breeze's Towers]].

The pouch of stones is commonly used by assassins from [[Shai]].
