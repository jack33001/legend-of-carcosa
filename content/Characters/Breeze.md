A travelling merchant selling very unusual wares. We sold the [[The Slagged Blade]] to him, and now want it back. 

On our way back from [[Arku’s Bones]], we watched as [[The Mistress of War]], [[The Lord of Scribes]], and an unknown lord tortured him. From this, we can determine he is immortal (or at least *very* durable).
