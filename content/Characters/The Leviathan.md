A gigantic machine, used to lay seige to [[Ny'al]]. The machine was constructed by [[The Red Quarter Rebels]] with the help of [[The Lord of Puppets]]; [[The Blackstars]] fetched its heart. During [[The Sunrise Seige]], it was destroyed by [[The Blackstars]].
