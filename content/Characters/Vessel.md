Vessels are a result of the banishment of gods to alternate planes during [[The Age of Apostasy]]. The ability for souls to move on to wherever they needed was severed, so when a person dies, their soulless body is ripe for control by beings from other planes. A vessel can take any shape or size; the only constant is that the more souls a vessel consumes, the more powerful it will be.
