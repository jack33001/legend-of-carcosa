Full name "Capofamiglia Vico Tartuffo", [[Gino]]’s father and the head of [[L’ombra Profonda (The Tartuffo Family)]], Vico is a calculating and ruthless patriarch. Vico embodies the mafia’s core philosophy: family loyalty above all, no matter the cost. He believes in the Tartuffo legacy as an inheritance to be defended and expanded, even if it means sacrificing lives or courting eldritch powers. Vico raised [[Gino]] with a strict and unforgiving love, indoctrinating him into the family’s values.
