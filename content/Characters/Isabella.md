Full name Isabella “Bella” Tartuffo, [[Gino]]’s daughter, raised by [[Vico]] after he left. Isabella has been groomed by the family as an assassin, taught that her father is a traitor responsible for her mother’s death. Nicknamed “[[La Rosa Nera]]” (“The Black Rose”), she is a deadly force within [[L’ombra Profonda (The Tartuffo Family)]], embodying the family’s values in a way that both terrifies and saddens [[Gino]], who yearns to explain his side of the story to her.
