Orders for capturing this vessel were found at [[The Teeth]].

This vessel is described as red, sick, humaniod, and gangly for its small size. It loves gambling; the paper gives explicit details for trapping not to try bargaining or gambling. Divine iconography is tattooed on its skin, pertaining to a god of misfortune.
