**Groups:** [[The Traveling Liches]], [[The Gilded Elves]]

**Characters:** [[Da Lo Lensky]], [[Nara]]

**Locations:** [[Carcosa]]

------------------------------------------------------------------------

A mysterious traveler who joined [[Da Lo Lensky]] and [[Nara]] on their travels; he taught the two how to become liches. Though the two became imperfect liches, the traveler himself was a perfect lich. One day, upon seeing a statue of an old god of [[The Gilded Elves]], the traveler became angry, and told [[Da Lo Lensky]] and [[Nara]] that he never wished to see them again.
