# Session Summary
Walking into the next room, it's filled with dead bodies and barnacley growths
- Against a wall is a silhouette of a person, legs blown off. They appear to be breathing still. The barnacles seem to explode upon contact with weight
	- The wounds of the dead all seem to have similar wounds, that almost look cauterized. Disintegration beam?
	- We wake him up, he's traumatized as hell. He says "the star will blind you" and "I will not tell you how to cross." His name is [[Barnaby]]
		- Charming him, we get some more info:
			- The barnacles recede when they touch water
			- The next room holds the heart of the star
			- We can lure it to shoot in the direction of the sunken chapel
- [[Adder]] sprays the barnacles with fresh water and we cross to the next room (we sleep [[Barnaby]])
The next hallways pitches deep down into the ground, the walls are hewn with purple and green veins. The walls appear calcified. The hallways opens up into a big, strange open area. There are six hills (points of the star?), and a deep hole in the middle. The area has no water, it seems far away the water is held at bay somehow. PIllartown appears to be built atop one of the tentacles, the sunken chapel atop another. The other four don't reach up as far, and lay resting. 
- [[Etron]] flies his bird above the hole
	- The bottom of the hole glows green
	- A purple/pink beam shoots out of the hole and disintegrates the bird
	- The four resting tentacles curl up above the party in a gigantic cage
	- [[Etron]] messages the ground and the hole, finding two entities
		- Suddenly, we all have a vision and are able to see the entire starfish in a bird's-eye view. It's surrounded by shattered ships and destroyed cities. 
		- A smaller version of the starfish raises from the hole ([[The Star Psion]])
			- This fight was brutal
