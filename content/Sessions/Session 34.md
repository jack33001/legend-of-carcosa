# Session Summary
- Session resumed in the middle of the previous fight
	- After clearing out many pirates, a [[Porcelain Soldier]] similar to [[The Prefect]] is powered on and attacks us
	- As [[The Blackstars]] fought, the crystals in the room began to glow and the pit in the center of the filled with water
		- Eventually, [[Roto]] teleports in with his boat, The Last Gambit
- [[Roto]] calls a ceasefire after the porcelain beast is destroyed
- After inspecting The Last Gambit, [[Ratpack]]'s cargo is nowhere to find
	- [[Roto]] claims there was no cargo, but changes his mind once [[Etron]] starts reading off the ledger [[Hen]] had stolen from his office
		- The ledger mentions [[Ludos]], [[The Rust Queen]], [[Colith]], [[Ratpack]], some names marked from [[The Camaraderie]]
	- [[Roto]] tries to offer to get the cargo by himself, but [[The Blackstars]] demand to go with him.
- [[The Blackstars]] split at this point - [[Gino]], [[Hen]], and [[Etron]]'s thrall stay behind to protect [[The Second Sunset]] while the rest of the party boards The Last Gambit to go with [[Roto]] and recover [[Ratpack]]'s cargo
- The teleporter leads to a small cave with some basic accommodations - this is clearly not the main base, as much as just the other side of the teleporter
	- [[Ratpack]]'s cargo is recovered
	- [[Etron]] walks out the entrance to see where they are; he sees they are in a cistern in the main canal of [[Carcosa]].
		- The marks from [[The King in Yellow]] tingle
- After returning back through the teleporter, [[The Blackstars]] head back out to sea with [[Ratpack]]'s cargo
	- [[The Blackstars]] open [[Ratpack]]'s cargo
		- Black Metal Locked Box: Contains four staves, that look like they originate from [[The Enlightened Quarter]]
		- White Locked Box: Three little amphoras ([[The Amphora]])
