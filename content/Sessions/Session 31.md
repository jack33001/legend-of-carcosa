# Session Summary
- By killing the fleshy pillar, we have opened several tunnels in the fleshy area
- Found a necrotic whip as a tendril inside the pillar - [Strand of Atrophy](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/9522118-strand-of-atrophy)
- Many of the tunnels eventually shrink into small and tight passages, except one where the temperature matches the outside
- After more travelling, about 60 feet above us is a large wound which marks an exit to the surface
- Touching this wound feels like another mind touching yours, it may be overwhelming to climb the brain of this creature
- Upon climbing the creature, the party is assaulted with visions from the creature's memory
	- This creature landed in a crater in the world and commanded to wreak destruction on this world. Hills and mountains are like shrubs - empires and cities crumble. Approach a city where men take up arms and gods die underneath (likely [[Carcosa]], it looks familiar). The creature crawls to a place to die. A leech makes a home on its grave ([[Da Lo Lensky]]), sucking the remaining life from the creature.
- The air is cold and brisk
- The party then makes its way up the shattered skull cap, we climb until we break through the clouds, on a small island in the clouds and see a massive sea of clouds and a tall tower made of rotted bone atop the creature
- The foyer in the tower is well decorated and nice - the entrance closed behind and another opened further in the tower
- An area is decorated like a dining room, but with no table - a table and chairs descend from the darkened ceiling
- The lich meets us and we sit for a discussion over food, his name is Da La Lensky
- “Abomination” - [[Molescere]] states as he sees the lich
- The creature has been here almost longer than the lich
- THe lich will only last as long as the large creature still has life, came shortly after [[The Age of Apostesy]]. 
- [[Etron]] mentions his thirst for knowledge, vestiges are remnants of the vessels which gods used to walk among us - he recommends striking a bargain while I have the chance
- It is not possible for a lich to sustain themself off of vessels, or the fluid found in vessels; [[Da Lo Lensky]] confirms that [[The Gold Liquid]] is something akin to a soul.
- [[Da Lo Lensky]] was born around here, over 9,000 years ago. When he was a child 2 or 3 centuries after [[The Age of Apostesy]], the world was a wreck. He traveled with a friend - a woman, [[Nara]] who was studying the land, they also met [[The Tattered Traveler]] who traveled with them, who was a true lich. He helped them become liches. [[Da Lo Lensky]] told us a story of [[Carcosa]], and a temple dedicated to the gold of [[The Gilded Elves]] - the true lich became angry upon seeing it and told [[Nara]] and [[Da Lo Lensky]] he wished never to see them again.
- Told to deliver a box to [[Nara]] in [[The Great Ridge]] in the far north, if she has passed we may keep it.
- He has also extended the lesson of spell weaving
- Knows little of the Yellow King
- False liches were far more common pre-[[The Age of Apostesy]]
- [[Da Lo Lensky]] teleports us back to [[The Barge]]
- Leave the corpse beast
- Take watch on an Island we moor at - feels as we are being watched
- Next day uneventful, as the night ends we smell the sea, about only a day away
- The following night, the hags came to default on their bargain. When we refused to offer up something of great importance to us (they feed on despair), they attacked; [[The Blackstars]] were victorious. It is likely [[The Coven of the Maw]] intended for us to fight [[Da Lo Lensky]] and perish at his hand, such that they could consume the despair produced.
