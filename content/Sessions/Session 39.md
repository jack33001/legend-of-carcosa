	- The walls and floor are made of an odd glistening material, strange intertwining tubular shapes

		- There's some old, torn up leather and armor scraps strewn among the tubes.

	- Shapes can be seen darting through the water
	- Far down the tunnel, something pearlescent can be seen in the walls
	- There are a few corpses of [[The Rust Queen]]'s goons at the entrance, and some dead cultists further down
		- Ripping the clothes off a cultist, their upper body is humanoid, and their heads open into a sea star with a toothy maw. The lower body is a mass of tentacles, which look similar to the previous room
	- [[Gino]] steps into the tunnel, and a cultist immediately bursts from the far wall, grabs him, and jumps in the other side to begin swimming away
		- The cultist seems to be swimming towards a gigantic, pearlescent Nautilus. We can surmise that the cultists want to feed the creature
		- It was a very rough fight
- Further down the corridor, a glowing light alternates between green, blue, and purple. Numerous cadavers, with cauterized missing limbs, are strewn about the tunnel.
