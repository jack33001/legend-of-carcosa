# Session Summary
- Session begins with a standoff between [[The Blackstars]] and [[The Kirrasa]] at [[Aphun's Tower]]
	- Etron had just flown up into the tower
- After the fight, we find a note and some pendants
	- The note says the last person they bought the '[[Meridian]]' from was a wizard named [[Aphun]] in [[Pillartown]]
- Enter the tower
	- On the second floor, there is a ~15 foot diameter sphere floating above a brass pedestal in the center of the room. It's engraved with faintly glowing teal runes that move over its surface
	- On the third floor, we meet [[Aphun]] and his mage. 
		- Asked [[Aphun]] why [[The Kirrasa]] were attacking his tower
			- He said they were there to exact revenge; a previous group of Kirrasa had gone to the east and he had given them a device to get past an obstacle there. The group didn't return due to being 'ill prepared', but [[The Kirrasa]] thought he had given faulty goods.
		- Asked [[Aphun]] what the big brass ball is
			- Says he found it in ancient ruins to the far southwest; it's the object of his study, but won't share more about it
			- When [[Etron]] asks [[Molescere]], he says "it's a tool for fools and apostates. Use it and you will get lost."
		- Asked [[Aphun]] what [[The Kirrasa]] were after
			- He says there are some ancient ruins out that way.
		- Asked [[Aphun]] what the [[Meridian]] is
			- The service/device he sells
			- It grants protection and aids in ship navigation
		- Asked [[Aphun]] if he knows about [[Montez]]'s rocks
			- Seen most commonly in possession of assassins from [[Shai]]
		- Asked [[Aphun]] if he knows [[Breeze]] or any liches
			- Doesn't think any liches exist anymore
			- [[Breeze]] is a familiar name; a few hundred years ago he may have run intro a trader who uses that name
		- [[Aphun]] offers us a [[Meridian]]
			- Each one is unique and confers unpredictable things, but assists with navigation and provides safety for ships.
			- He sold the last one for ~5k gp
		- Asked [[Aphun]] if we can use his teleportation circle
			- He says we'd need to strike much more of a deal
- Walking back from the tower, we see two people fall from the sway into the sea below; they don't survive the drop
- Return to the ship
	- Dragged one of [[The Kirrasa]] of the ship to interrogate
