# Session Summary
Resume just after the first bug fight
- Just down the hall, we can see a slight glow coming from another corridor
- Out the corridor is a new room, with a deep hole/pit in the center
	- The walls are lined with faintly orange-glowing larvae
	- Some bugs are hiding among the larvae, presumably in wait for us
	- We fight
- After the fight, we figure out that the queen is at the bottom of the pit (along with a bunch of bugs); they're waiting for us
	- We kill em all
After the fight, we look around the chamber. Inside, we find [[Remembrance Lost]], and some old shipping documents talking about two glowing blue orbs ([[Reverie]]s?) that were shipped to [[Zuk]]

Taking watch that night, [[Jeraime]] notices some odd bubbles in the water; they disappear by the time she mentions them to [[Gattenk]]

We go get a reward from [[The Rust Queen]], and do some shopping

We leave for one of [[Breeze's Towers]] (near [[Shai]]); after about a day of sailing, an enormous shadow moves below the ship
