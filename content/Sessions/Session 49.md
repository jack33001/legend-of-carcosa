# Session Summary
- Arriving at [[The Second Sunset]], we find a crowd around the boat
	- Pushing our way to the front, we see that our boat has been totally ransacked
	- A single stool sits on the deck with a note, sealed in silver wax; a single pebble sits on the stool (the skeletons, [[Nos]], and [[Athelia]] are gone)
		- God damn [[Montez]]
		- The note says to come to the silver palace, and that our friends will not be harmed if we come to the palace in the next day
	- We take a long rest on the boat
- In the morning, [[Etron]] casts Identify on the pebble - it's nonmagical, but appears to have a little silver lodestone inside. When [[Adder]] tosses it in the air and thinks of [[Montez]], it pulls in the direction of the city
- Heading to [[The Silver Palace]], we feel like we're being watched
	- Coming upon the gates, we're escorted inside by an unusually large number of guards
	- Entering the throne room, there are seven chairs, only three of which are filled. A robed figure in a silver mask sits in one - [[The Overseer of Shai]]
		- The guards stand surprisingly far behind us, to the extent that one of us could run to the overseer and attack before they caught us
	- [[The Overseer of Shai]] tells us:
		- After we killed [[The Lord of Beauty]], [[Carcosa]] sent out a missive calling for [[The Blackstars]] to be captured and brought to [[Carcosa]], or killed
		- A mysterious illness has befallen [[Shai]], and [[The Overseer of Shai]] has reason to believe it's cause by something in the water.
			- He presents us a deal - either he turns us in to [[The King in Yellow]], or he locks us in the Aqueduct to figure out the cause of this illness and tells [[The King in Yellow]] that he's captured us. If we solve the problem and escape, he won't pursue us to our boat and will owe us a debt.
	- [[Gino]] follows his compass
		- The path winds through [[Shai]]
		- It leads to a large, strange building - each little segment of the building looks like it's in a completely different architectural style. It's a cathedral, built of churches from all over the world ([[The Cathedra of Glass]])
			- Inside the cathedra, the compass is really starting to swing around - it leads to a yellow corner dedicated to the zealots of [[The King in Yellow]]
				- Specifically, it points to one bald, middle-aged man in a white robe
					- [[Phari]] talks to him ([[Phari]] doesn't have the king's mark)
						- He's very zealous, and does nothing but sell his religion - [[Phari]] upsets him and he says "your curiosity is like a [[The Blackstars|black star]] that blocks the golden light". When he says this, his pupils morph into crosses for just a moment.
	- We head to [[The House of Veins]]
		- Stepping inside, the place is a mess. There are bloodied bandages everywhere, and patients covered in bleeding sores and retching blood are in beds all over
		- The person in charge tells us that for months, people have been succumbing to this strange illness. They've determined it's in the water, and after [[Etron]] cast Identify, we've determined there to be some sort of magic going on in the water. Casting destroy water, a tiny amount of liquid like spoiled blood remains. 
- We head back to [[The Second Sunset]] and take a rest
- We head back to [[The Silver Palace]], ready to delve into [[The Aqueducts of Shai]]
	- [[The Overseer of Shai]] leads us up some stairs to a balcony. From here, we can see snowy plains ~40 miles north. This is odd - it's about 70 degrees here. [[Adder]] recalls that this is actually not snow, but ash, that has been falling since [[The Age of Apostasy]]
	- We're led further, to a giant stone door controlled with two chains. Inside, numerous cells full of prisoners line the walls. [[Adder]] recalls that [[Shai]] uses [[The Aqueduct Prison]] for its worst criminals; the door closes behind us.
