# Session Summary
- We have crossed over the dome, and we are slowly making our way up the hillside while being watched by small vessels hounding the corpse of the vessel we fought
- We crest the hill and watch dawn break over the fields and city of [[Carcosa]], an idyllic scene betraying the mystery of the place. The color which shines from the cities central tower is eerily similar to the color of the liquid in [[The Amphora]].
- While taking a long rest, the bandit we had in the caravan escaped. Hen tracked the man to a farm on the hillside with a trapdoor underneath and a weird stone basement and an odd symbol on an oaken door.    
- We enter the room - small room with a small mirror giving off magical presence and a small dart trap. The magical mirror has a small inscription on the bottom back “Darken the sun, brighten the slight” - A mirror of sending and the inscription is the activation phrase. But the mirror was destroyed as the man at the mirror on the other side smashed his side.
- We stop in the city, receive our payment and spend some money on items.
- We travel to [[The Seraphim]], help and heal some of the inhabitants. About 9 were injured in some occurrence between the grove and billows
- Described as being eaten by an invisible entity
- "Part of living in Carcosa”
- Talked to the main doctor, Zelca, described by the [[The Beholden]] as the “insolent” or the Casapear, they investigate the disappearances in [[Carcosa]].
- People who live in Carcosa for an extended period of time seem to lose the ability to see vessels - avoid the food grown in the city
- Told to travel to [[The Joy]] an inn ran by [[Carphius]]
- Assistant to doctor, [[Gurney]], told us to deliver a sack with an item (told not to look at) to a small house with a small red door in [[The Billows]], knock 4 times and tell them “Darken the sun, brighten the slight”
- Additionally, the ‘Parade of Lords’ is to occur tomorrow around the city    
- Asked by [[Carphius]] to examine the contents of a nearby building
- In the building two cages each with a decrepit person, seem to be tortured by someone, mentioned a man with white hair and orange eyes.
- In the basement of the building, a manhole is the entrance to a sewer-like area, taking you to a pile of bodies inside the sparkling tower
