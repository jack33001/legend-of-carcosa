# Session Summary
- Begin by looking into [[The Sunken Choir]] and [[The Sunken Basilica]]
	- People seem to react like it's not something you're supposed to talk about
- [[The Blackstars]] go to investigate the inside of the church, as [[Gattenk]] looks for [[The White Fish]]
	- They send one of [[Etron]]'s skeleton's down the a hole to explore
- Return to [[The Second Sunset]] to talk with [[Athelia]] about some water breathing potions
- Head down the tunnels
- Come out to a fully sumberged pier, with a hooded silent figure on it
	- He demands we pay the toll
		- 200 gold does the job
	- The compass points deeper into the water
- We sit in the figure's ferry, and it moves deeper into the water
- Eventually, the ferry brings us to a bubble of air, in which a "concrete" platform lined with green burning torches sits
- The platform runs into a narrow gorge, with some sort of a constructed structure at the end of it
- Entering the structure, we find a circle of members of [[The Sunken Choir]] humming; they don't seem to notice us
	- [[Adder]] tries to look at one of their faces, and they get clearly upset, but resume humming when he looks away
- Trails of blood and flakes of skin litter the path
- We come to a room with a bowl in it, with a flame inside. There are similar murals on the walls, with words similar to those that had been chanting in [[The Sunken Basilica]].
	- Offering blood and lemon ginger tea (from Polly's Flask), we aren't able to get any reaction
- The next room has rusted iron cages piled up the walls
	- In one of the cages (door open), one of the hooded figures sits in front of a star of driftwood, humming
		- This one will speak to us - he asks us if we're bringing an offering
			- [[The Choir Member's Wish]]
