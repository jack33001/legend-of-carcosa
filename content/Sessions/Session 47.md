# Session Summary
- We tell [[Nos]] and [[Athelia]] to sail up to [[Shai]] as we try to track the assassins
- The drow prints stop pretty quickly, but a smaller figure's (presumed to be a [[L’ombra Profonda (The Tartuffo Family)|Tartuffo]]) continue
- We track these prints for a full day, and spot an unusual set of ruins off the path of the tracks
	- We take a short diversion to check out the ruins
	- There are four arches roughly in the cardinal directions with crumbling lettering, and an empty foundation in the middle
		- "Drown these old stones in the pale..."
		- [[Etron]] detects illusion magic everywhere
		- [[Phari]] casts moonbeam upon the foundation, and it opens up a set of stairs
		- [[Molescere]] says "This is another grave for dead ideas"
	- An archway with a glowing purple moon emblem is at the bottom of the stairs, with some sort of magical barrier. When any part of the body touches the barrier, it feels like that part of the body is falling asleep
		- As everyone tries a bunch of esoteric methods of dissolving the barrier, [[Jeraime]] just dispels magic
	- Through the door is a large sepulchre - inside, [[Phari]] finds a glowing purple moon. It feels familiar
- Following the tracks, they eventually lead to a small hamlet built in the ruins of an old fortress
	- [[Gino]] convinces a farmer to tell him where the man we're tracking is hiding
	- She says he's in a storehouse - knock four times to see him
	- [[Gino]]'s compass has been pointing directly behind [[The Blackstars]] for this whole session - eventually, he realizes they're being tailed
	- [[Phari]] casts See Invisibility and spots six drow (wearing the same armor as the dead drow we saw at the tower) watching us. Upon being spotted, they immediately attack
