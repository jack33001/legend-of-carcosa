Continuing the fight with the [[The Lord of the Hunt]]

- We can see the [[Meridian]] is working - a green aurora is glowing thirty feet out from the ship, and we can hear the faintest of songs 
- A pile of shipwreck and wood sits in the rocks
	- [[Etron]] sends his bird out of the bubble, and immediately loses control of it - it flies towards the wreck.

		- They find a cave, with a stone box containing a [[Reverie]]!
