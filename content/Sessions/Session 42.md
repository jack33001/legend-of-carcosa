# Session Summary
[[Hen]] and [[Etron]] head back into the ship - the others stay outside and keep track of the time
- The boat is just kinda wrong - it feels like it was made by a person who was just imagining what a boat is
- [[Etron]] finds some illusion magic going on, which he dispels
	- A door is revealed
- [[Hen]] tries to chip up some of the wood floor, to no avail
Through the door, a sparse captains office
- The desk is full of things that feel like a random person would imagine to be in a captains desk, but they don't make much sense
	- There's a pile of blank, non-square paper
[[Hen]] notices there is a fresh scab on the back of his neck; [[Etron]] has the same thing
- They go back to the party and inform them; everyone has these scabs
Everyone but [[Adder]] and [[Gino]] go back to investigate the boat, leaving the rope tied back up
- [[Gattenk]] takes an axe to a rope, and the rope shatters
- [[Phari]] dispels the illusion magic; the boat is revealed to be a stone imitation of a boat. It's held together by some sort of resin
- A clawed-out tunnel is revealed, filled with insect-humanoid creatures
	- [[Etron]] recognizes these as a type of creature from [[Shai]], which establish nests and feed on populations
After a small fight, [[Gino]] and [[Adder]] are brought to the boat, to exterminate the nest

In the first room, there are a few bugs sitting in a circle. One is wearing a shoddy cloak. 
- We fight them, and reinforcements swarm in from the walls. If one of these creatures uses its proboscis on a character, that character will attack whatever ally walks by next.
