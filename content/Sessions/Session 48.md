# Session Summary
- After the fight, [[Etron]] searches for (and finds) the ring belonging to the assassin that he turned into an ecroche
	- Casting identify, it's a Band of Armament (a ring that can turn into a selection of +1 weapons)
- [[Hen]] ties up the guy who's been knocked unconscious
- [[Gino]] and [[Phari]] stay with the tied up guy, while the rest of [[The Blackstars]] head over to the storehouse to find the gnome
	- He looks in pretty bad shape - his bandaged leg is far smaller than the other, as if it lost a lot of flesh
	- When [[Hen]] asks, he claims not to have the key from [[Breeze's Towers|Breeze's Tower]], but [[Hen]] hears him lugging something around in there
- We interrogate the captured drow
	- He tells us that we shouldn't meddle in affairs outside our concern, then turns his ring into a weapon and kills himself
	- [[Etron]] casts Gentle Repose on the drow, stabilizing the body. Gino cuts off the hand with the ring on it and hands it to [[Etron]] as if shaking hands, and says "Nice to meet you". [[Etron]] laughs for the first time.
	- The hand poofs into smoke, but the body remains
- The non socially retarded party members then head back to the gnome to talk again
	- [[Etron]] immediately picks up that he was lying about carrying something
	- Asking what job he was doing
		- He says he was looking for artifacts in nearby ruins
	- Pushing him and telling him we know it was a [[L’ombra Profonda (The Tartuffo Family)|Tartuffo]] job, he still doesn't budge
	- [[Jeraime]]'s turn
		- He admits it was a [[L’ombra Profonda (The Tartuffo Family)|Tartuffo]] job in particular
		- He says he found nothing, but [[Jeraime]]'s insight roll says otherwise
	- He gives us the key (with some persuasion) and says he's a dead man if he returns without it
		- As we leave, he tells us that [[Vico]] has been collecting vessels, and it's endangering the people of [[Sanstrelli]]
- We pay the townspeople some money before heading out to help cover the damage we did
	- The woman we gave some money to tells us that, if we're heading to [[Shai]], we should avoid the lowlands
- We walk an hour or two north, and set up Leomund's Tiny Hut to sleep in a small clearing
- [[Gino]] and [[Phari]] hear something that sounds like the whispers of cackling on the distant wind. Not quite like the witches we faced - more like an animal or monster laughing
- The next day, we continue heading northward. The terrain consists of a number of north-south running slot canyons; we heed the villager's warning, and stay on the top of the canyons
- We find a small camp, and pull up to say hello. They hesitantly welcome us, but there seems to be a tension in the air (not from us, but something that was there already)
	- [[Etron]] asks about it
		- There's a sister caravan headed for [[Duave]] that couldn't take the highlands - they were supposed to meet up on this hill, but they haven't shown up yet. They were lost somewhere nortwest, in the direction of [[Shai]]
- The next morning, the caravan is packing up ready to leave
- We pack up and head northwest towards [[Shai]]
- In the late afternoon, [[Gino]] sees the colors of the rainbow briefly flash over the horizon. A little while after, we hear the cackling again coming from roughly the same direction
	- Reaching that area, we see some odd shapes in the lowlands. 
	- We head down, and find the ruined caravan
	- The bodies strewn about look like they've been ruptured from the inside; shatter, perhaps?
	- From somewhere nearby, we hear the cackling again
	- [[Gino]] and [[Hen]] quietly go to track the creature. It looks like a twisted quadrupedal beast, covered in multicolored dots. It's like a greyhound, but with a thick, sagging neck that inflates like a frog and a head consisting almost entirely of a toothy maw. As it breathes, it incessantly cackles. It's pretty big, and looks to be heading northwest (towards [[Shai]]) with intent. 
- We take a rest in Leomund's tiny hut and the vessel approaches, seemingly attracted by the light
	- [[Phari]] turns off the light, and the vessel leaves
- The next day, we track the creature to a unique ravine with a few huge overhangs; we see it curled up against a wall as if sleeping
