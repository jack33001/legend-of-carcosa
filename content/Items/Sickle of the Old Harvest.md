You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

**Sickle of the Old Harvest** _Weapon (scythe), rare (requires attunement)_

This ominous scythe is crafted from a cobbled mixture of black and white metal plates, with a long jagged curved blade that faintly glows red. Yellow traces of light run up the handle to the base of the blade.

**Harvesting Blade:** The scythe deals an additional 1d4 necrotic damage on a successful hit.

**Severing the Bonds that Bind:** Once per turn, striking an undead creature that you control heals you for all the damage you did to that creature.
