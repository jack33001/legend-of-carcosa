The item was found in [[Session 12]] after defeating the [[Mandragora]] at the top of the [[Seafoam Tower]] in [[Nyopsis]].

This item is currently in the possession of [[The Blackstars]] and resides inside of [[The Second Sunset]], serving as a teleportation circle in the vessel.

_Wondrous Item, very rare (requires attunement)_

This ancient magic carpet is intricately embroidered with glowing runes, with a teleportation circle woven into its center. It radiates an aura of distant lands and untraveled paths, calling to those with a heart for exploration.

**Properties:**

**Teleportation Circle:** Once per day, as an action, you can activate the teleportation circle embroidered in the center of the carpet. The circle functions as the _Teleportation Circle_ spell, allowing you to transport yourself and up to eight willing creatures (standing on the carpet) to a location with a permanent teleportation circle that you are familiar with. This use recharges at dawn. This carpet can also be used as the destination point of a teleportation circle spell.
