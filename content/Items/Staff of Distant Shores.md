_Staff, rare (requires attunement by a spellcaster)_

This staff is made from sun-bleached, pockmarked, dried coral and resonates with the power of distant seas.

**Spellcasting Focus:** The staff can be used as a spellcasting focus for your spells.

**Amphibious Nature:** While attuned to the staff, you gain the ability to breathe underwater, and you have a swimming speed equal to your walking speed.

**Tidal Surge:** Once per long rest, you can use an action to summon a tidal surge. Each creature in a 30-foot cone must make a DC 15 Strength saving throw, taking 3d8 bludgeoning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. Creatures that fail the saving throw are also pushed 15 feet away from you.

**Aquatic Empowerment:** The staff has 3 charges. As an action, you can expend a charge to cast one of the following spells without using a spell slot: _Create Water (1 charge_), _Water Walk_ (2 charges). The staff regains all expended charges daily at dawn. All spells are casted at their lowest level.
