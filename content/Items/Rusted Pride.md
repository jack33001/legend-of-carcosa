_Staff, rare (requires attunement)_

This dark iron staff features a subliminal sleek design covered with an ever-persistent vibrant rust. When attuned to Rusted Pride, you gain the following benefits:

- **Unbroken Pride:** Rusted Pride has a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls.
    
- **Inescapable Corrosion:** The last creature struck by a ranged spell attack made with Rusted Pride as the focus will suffer a -1 AC penalty.
    
- **Vibrant Decay:** Once per short rest, as an action, you can channel the staffs energy into a ranged attack. On a hit for any **ranged** spell attack, the target takes 1d8 acid damage and must succeed on a Constitution saving throw (DC 15) or have its AC reduced by 1d4 until the end its next turn. This effect can stack on top of **Inescapable Corrosion**
    
- **Tenacious Affliction:** When you score a critical hit with Rusted Pride or a ranged spell attack made with Rusted Pride, the target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw (DC 15) or have its armor class reduced by 1d6 as the rust begins to eat away at its protective layers. This reduction lasts for 1 minute and can stack on top of **Vibrant Decay** and **Inescapable Corrosion**.
    

Proficiency with a Quarterstaff allows you to add your proficiency bonus to the attack roll for any attack you make with it.
