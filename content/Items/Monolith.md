_Weapon (greataxe), rare (requires attunement)_

This massive double-headed war axe is carved from a porous, dark stone, and its edges are adorned with intricate gold embossing. When attuned to Monolith, you gain the following benefits:

- **Stone's Fury:** Monolith has a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls. Additionally, on a critical hit, the target takes an additional 1d6 bludgeoning damage and is knocked prone as the sheer weight and force of the axe crush through armor and resistance.
    
- **Unsettling Presence:** Monolith's massive size and weight grants you advantage on intimidation checks.
    
- **Earthshaker:** Once per long rest, you can slam Monolith into the ground with tremendous force. All creatures within a 10-foot radius must make a Strength saving throw (DC 15) or be knocked prone and take 6d6 thunder damage. This ability also deals 12d6 bludgeoning damage to structures or objects in the area.
    
- **Weight of the Monolith:** If you beat the AC of a target by 5 or more you may choose to shove them.
    
- **Magical Momentum:** This weapon can be thrown as if it had the thrown weapon property. It can be magically recalled to your hand as a bonus action.

You have a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.
