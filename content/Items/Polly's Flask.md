_Wondrous Item (canteen), rare (requires attunement)_

This large metal flask, scratched and dented from countless adventures, holds the unpredictable magic of Polly's whimsy. While attuned to Polly's Flask, you gain the following benefits

- **Manifestation of Libations:** Once per day, as an action, you can summon a random liquid within Polly's Flask. Roll a d6 on the table below to determine the liquid's type:
    
    1. **Elixir of Vigor:** The drinker gains advantage on Strength saving throws for 1 hour.
    2. **Lunar Nectar:** The drinker gains darkvision with a range of 60 feet for 1 hour.
    3. **Sparkling Brew:** The drinker gains advantage on Dexterity saving throws for 1 hour.
    4. **Velvet Essence:** The drinker gains advantage on Charisma-based skill checks for 1 hour.
    5. **Mystic Tonic:** The drinker gains advantage on Intelligence saving throws for 1 hour.
    6. **Ephemeral Elixir:** The drinker gains advantage on Wisdom saving throws for 1 hour.
- **Refreshing Rejuvenation:** When you finish a long rest while attuned to Polly's Flask and you have at least 1 hit point, you regain an additional 1d6 hit points as the canteen's magic invigorates you.
    
- **Bottle of Many Surprises:** Once per week, Polly's Flask can manifest a particularly unusual liquid. The DM can determine the effect based on the needs of the story or roll on a unique table for unpredictable outcomes.
