_Wondrous Item (sitar and instrument bow), very rare (requires attunement)_

_"Soothing sound stitches and seals all scars"_

**Description:**  
Twine and Needle is a paired magical sitar and instrument bow, both crafted from ancient dark wood adorned with bronze inlay. The bronze detailing takes the shape of scales, giving the instruments a serpent-like appearance. When played, Twine and Needle resonate with ethereal, magical melodies.

**Instrumental Magic (Once per day):**  
As an action, Twine and Needle can play one of the following magical tunes once per day. The magic of the music affects all who can hear it within a 30-foot radius.

- **Overture:**  
    _Effect:_ All allies within 30 feet of you who can hear the music gain 10 temporary hit points for 1 minute. These hit points are resistant to all forms of damage.  
    _Duration:_ 1 minute.  
      
    
- **Crashing Creshendo:**  
    _Effect:_ All enemies within 30 feet who can hear the music must make a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, they take 1d4 psychic damage, are deafened for 1 minute, and the user regains HP equal to the damage dealt. On a successful save, they take no damage.  
    _Duration:_ Instant.  
      
    
- **Suture Sonata:**  
    _Effect:_ All allies within 30 feet who can hear the music regain hit points equal to 1/5 (20%) of their missing HP.  
    _Duration:_ Instant.
