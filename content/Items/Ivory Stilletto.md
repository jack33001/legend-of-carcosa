_Weapon (dagger), uncommon (requires attunement)_

_"This thing slides into people like it fits"_

You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

**Critical Puncture.** On a Critical Hit, the target has to make a DC 13 constitution check or become paralyzed for 5 rounds making a save to end it early at the end of each round. 

**Murder With Finesse.** This weapon's critical hit threshold is 19.

This dagger is carved from the bone of a mysterious creature with paralyzing properties. Its handle its wrapped in black cloth and its blade is a shiny almost metallic like bone substance.
