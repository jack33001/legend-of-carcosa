A silver chisel, with veins of gold running through it. Stolen from [[L’ombra Profonda (The Tartuffo Family)]] at [[The Teeth]]. The box it was found in was protected with Power Word Kill, which killed [[Etron]] in [[Session 32]].

Under the correct ritual, given the correct catalyst, a [[Vessel]] can be bound for servitude or destruction. 
1. Dip the chisel into the catalyst
2. Engrave runes of control or destruction into the flesh of a vessel
The vessels seen by [[Gino]] and [[Hen]] at [[The Teeth]] were covered in scars, implying use of the chisel.
