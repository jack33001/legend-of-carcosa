_Wondrous Item, rare (requires attunement)_

_"Bear down on them with the weight of your wrath"_

This frightening shoulderplate takes the form of a blood-stained wood and dark rusted metal ram's head. The Mantle of the Ram exudes an ancient and ominous aura, empowering its wearer with the strength and fury of the ram.

- **Ram's Resilience:** The Mantle of the Ram provides you with additional protection. While wearing the mantle, you gain a +1 bonus to your AC.
    
- **Charge of the Ram:** As a bonus action, you can channel the spirit of the ram and gain a 30-foot increase to your movement speed until the end of your turn. During this turn, you can make a special charge attack. If you move at least 20 feet straight toward a target and then hit it with a melee attack on the same turn, the target takes an additional 2d6 bludgeoning damage. Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.
