**Longsword of Old Carcosa** _Weapon (longsword), rare (requires attunement)_

This longsword is crafted from smooth white metal, and faint chamfered traces of yellow light course through the handle, guard, and blade. It is said to be infused with the mysterious energy of an ancient realm.

**Superior Craftsmanship: This is a +1 Longsword.**

**Mystic Illumination:** The longsword sheds dim light in a 10-foot radius. As a bonus action, you can command the sword to intensify or dim the light, or to extinguish it altogether.

**Otherworldly Strike:** Twice per Long rest, when you hit a creature with the Longsword of Old Carcosa, you can choose to deal an additional 3d6 radiant damage.

**Carcosan Ward:** While attuned to the longsword, you have advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened.
