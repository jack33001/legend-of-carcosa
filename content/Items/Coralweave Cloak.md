_Wondrous item (mantle), rare (requires attunement)_

This once elegant mantle has been consumed by the essence of a giant eldritch crab, transforming it into a living piece of underwater malevolence.

_**Eldritch Infusion.**_ The Coralweave has absorbed the eldritch energy of the giant crab, granting it additional properties. While attuned to the Coralweave Cloak, you gain the following benefits:

- **Coral Camouflage.** You have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made while near water of non negligible depth. 
    
- **Coral Resilience.** You gain resistance to acid damage.
    
- **Eldritch Empowerment.** As a reaction to taking damage, you can release a burst of eldritch energy, dealing 2d6 force damage to all creatures within a 10-foot radius. The damage increases to 3d6 at the start of your next turn if you do not take any damage during that turn. Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.
