_Wondrous Item (bracers), rare (requires attunement)_

These enchanted brass bracers are intricately adorned with arcane symbols and emit a faint, otherworldly glow. When attuned to the Forearms of The Spelltouched, you gain the following benefits:

- **Spellweaver's Defense:** The Forearms of The Spelltouched grant a +1 bonus to AC when the wearer is not wearing armor.
    
- **Spell Resonance:** Once per long rest, the wearer can absorb the energy of a spell that targets them and store it within the bracers. The stored energy can be released as a reaction to add 1d6 damage to the wearer's next successful melee attack. Ranged spell attacks now count as projectiles to you.
    
- **Spelltouched Mastery:** Once per long rest, the wearer can cast _detect magic_ without expending a spell slot but only on an item they can touch.
