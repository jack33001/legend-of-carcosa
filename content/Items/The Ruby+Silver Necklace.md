A silver necklace, manufactured by [[The Roecrin Family]] originally intended as a gift from [[The Lords of Carcosa]] to [[The Ny'alkosh Family]]. Upon being put on by a member of [[The Ny'alkosh Family]], it would be irremovable, and would slowly poison the wearer over many years (30+). It was stolen by [[Kor]], then later retrieved by [[The Blackstars]].
