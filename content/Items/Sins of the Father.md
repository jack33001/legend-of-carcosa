_"I am the night that sets your sun"  

_Weapon (dagger), rare (requires attunement)_

This thick and staunch looking blade has strange runic engravings that are to scratched and illegible to be made out. The dark grey blade is fixed to a black wood handle wrapped in bloodstained cloth.

- **Cursed Edge:** Sins of the Father has a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls. Additionally, it deals an extra 1d4 necrotic damage on a successful hit.
    
- **Pass The Guilt:** When you land a critical hit with Sins of the Father, the target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw (DC 14) or be frightened until the end of their next turn.
    
- **Blood Bond:** As a bonus action, you can choose to draw blood with Sins of the Father. You regain hit points equal to the necrotic damage dealt by the dagger on your next successful attack, however you lose that much from your max HP until the end of your next long rest.
    
- **Veil of Shadows:** Once per short rest, you can use an action to envelop yourself in a shadowy aura. For 1 minute, you gain advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks and can attempt to hide even when only lightly obscured. Alternatively you can cast Darkness at its lowest level centered on yourself.
