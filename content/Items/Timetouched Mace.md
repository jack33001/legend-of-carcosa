_Weapon (Mace), Rare (Requires Attunement)_

_"Threads of destiny coil around the weapon, knitting past failures into inevitable success."_

**Description:**  
This weapon appears to have been infused with an alien, temporal energy. Its surface is infected with a burnished copper effect, twisting and spiraling in an organic yet otherworldly manner. The texture resembles the cellular structure of an unknown organism, and it emits a faint warmth, as if alive with the flow of time itself.

---

**Properties:**

- **+1 Weapon:** You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon.
    
- **Fated Feint:** Each time you miss an attack with this weapon during a single combat encounter, its bonus to attack rolls increases by +1. For example, if you miss once, you gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls. If you miss twice, it becomes a +2 bonus, and so on. This bonus resets to +0 at the start of a new combat.
    
- **Fateful Foresight:** Once per day, when you make a roll with disadvantage and fail, you can re-roll it as a normal roll (neither advantage nor disadvantage). You must use the new result.
