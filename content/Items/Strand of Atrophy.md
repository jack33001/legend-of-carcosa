_Weapon (Whip), Rare (Requires Attunement)_  
_"What once moved with grace now drags with despair."  
  
This whip is no more than a wriggling striated strand of ancient and cursed flesh, one that seeks to both indulge and defy the concept of decay.  

**Properties:**

- **+1 Weapon:** You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon.
    
- **Atrophic Decay:** When you hit a creature with this weapon, it takes an additional 1d4 necrotic damage. Additionally, until the end of its next turn, it takes 2 necrotic damage for every 5 feet it willingly moves.
    
- **Flesh That Binds:** If a creature afflicted by Atrophic Decay willingly moves during its turn, it must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw at the end of its turn. On a failed save, you can use your reaction to force the creature to move up to half its speed in a direction of your choice. This movement is considered forced movement and does not trigger Atrophic Decay.
    

**Curse: The Flesh is Fragile**  
Once attuned, this whip embeds itself into your forearm like a fleshy seed. you cannot unequip this whip unless you are targeted by a _Remove Curse_ spell or similar magic. Alternatively you can attempt a medicine roll to excise it from your flesh, suffering a permanent level of exhaustion for a period of time.  While attuned, your body becomes unnaturally delicate:

- You take 1 additional point of damage for every source of damage you receive. This damage bypasses resistances and immunities.
