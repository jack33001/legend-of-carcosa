_Wondrous Item (earpiece), uncommon (requires attunement)_

This long earpiece is crafted in the shape of a silver snake, its coiled body wrapping around the ear when worn. When clamped down, it inflicts a minor wound upon the wearer, but in doing so, neutralizes a poison affecting them. When attuned to the Snakebite Earband, you gain the following benefits:

- **Piercing Bite:** Once per day, as a bonus action, the wearer can clamp down the Snakebite Earband, inflicting 1 piercing damage to themselves. This action neutralizes any ongoing poison effect affecting the wearer.
    
- **Serpentine Ward:** The earband has a passive ability to detect the presence of poison within 10 feet of the wearer. The snake-shaped earpiece vibrates slightly when poison is detected.
    
- **Venomous Cleansing:** If the wearer is poisoned, they can use an action to voluntarily activate the Snakebite Earband, ending the poisoned condition.
    
- **Restorative Venom:** While attuned to the Snakebite Earband, the wearer gains 1 additional hit point at the end of each short rest.
