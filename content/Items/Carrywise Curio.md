The Carrywise Curio was obtained in [[Session 14]] as treasure after raiding a caravan in the dunes of [[Ny'al]].

This item is currently held by [[Etron]], and is used to hold several of the [[Ecorche]]. There has not been a successful retrieval of a magic item from the Carrywise Curio using the Surprise Contents feature.

_Wondrous Item, uncommon

This leather satchel is adorned with intricate red thread embroidery and secured with silver buckles. When attuned to Carrywise Curio, you gain the following benefits:

- **Chaotic Storage:** The interior of the satchel has an unpredictable extradimensional space, allowing it to hold up to 100 pounds of items without changing its outward appearance. Retrieving an item from the bag requires a bonus action.
    
- **Random Fetch:** Once per day, as a bonus action, you can reach into the Carrywise Curio and pull out a random mundane item. The item is determined by rolling on a trinket table or chosen by the DM.
    
- **Surprise Contents:** When you use the Random Fetch feature with the Carrywise Curio, there is a 1% chance that it will be a magical item of the DM's choice.
