The item was found in [[Session 6]] after fighting and looting [[Gimp]] on the journey to [[The Smuggler's Pit]].

This item is held by [[Gattenk]].

_"You would think the porcelain would shatter before the door did..."_

You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

Any mundane, non-magical _objects_ take double damage from this weapon.

This weapon is a large finely crafted axe head melded onto a long wooden shaft composed of dark polished wood and porcelain plates.
