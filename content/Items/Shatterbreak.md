_"I will not be the one left shattered"_

_Weapon (glaive), rare (requires attunement)_

This unique glaive is crafted from a raw beryllium-like material, giving it an appearance as though it has been shattered and fused back together. The Shatterbreak holds a mysterious power that hints at the potential to break apart once more. While attuned to the glaive, you gain the following benefits:

- **Shattered Edge:** You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic glaive.
    
- **Brittle Fragmentation:** Once per short rest, when you hit a creature with an attack using the glaive, you can unleash the volatile nature of the beryllium-like material. The target must make a Dexterity saving throw (DC 14) or take an additional 3d6 force damage as the glaive temporarily shatters upon impact. On a save the target takes half damage.
    
- **Reshaping Instability:** As a bonus action, you can resummon the Glaive if it has been shattered.
    
- **Fractured Resilience:** When you are hit by an attack (not a save), you can use your reaction to invoke the glaive's resilience. Roll a d4. On a roll of 1, the glaive absorbs some of the damage, reducing it by 1d10 and shatters. On a roll of 2-4, the glaive remains unchanged and you take full damage.
    
- **Unpredictable Reformation:** When you roll a natural 20 on an attack roll with Shatterbreak, the effects of **Brittle Fragmentation** occur without using up a charge or shattering the blade.
