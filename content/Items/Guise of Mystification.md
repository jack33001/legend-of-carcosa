This item was found in [[Session 8]] on a corpse at the foot of [[The Bastion]] by [[The Blackstars]] while they were searching for [[Kor]].

This item is held and attuned to by [[Gino]].

_You look but you do not see... That is the whole point."_

This ancient cloak elegantly billows with an orange and green metallic reflective sheen. The Guise of Mystification possesses the ability to befuddle and confound those who would seek to perceive its wearer.

**Cloak of Illusion:** As a bonus action, you can command the cloak to blend with its surroundings, granting you advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks for 1 minute. Once used, this feature cannot be used again until the next dawn.

**Illusory Doppelganger:** Once per long rest, as a reaction to being hit by an attack, you can use your reaction to create an illusory duplicate of yourself that takes the damage instead. The duplicate has the same AC as you but has only 1 hit point and is immediately dispelled after taking damage.

**Veil of Uncertainty:** Creatures that attempt to discern information about you through divination spells or effects must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw against a DC of 15 or receive false or misleading information.
