**Characters:**

**Locations:** [[Pillartown]], [[The Sunken Basilica]]

------------------------------------------------------------------------

A cult residing in [[Pillartown]]. They wear long, black robes; many of them are crusted in barnacles from wading in the water of [[The Sunken Basilica]] for so long. In [[The Sunken Basilica]], the choir members chant in a partially abyssal language about "the coming of the storm, thunderous return, hunger unsated".
