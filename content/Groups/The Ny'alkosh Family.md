[[The Ny'alkosh Family]] is the ruling dynasty of [[Ny'al]], and prides themselves on their bright blue scales. The color was a signal to every other noble house and peasant in the city, a symbol of their wealth and a clear way to distinguish the noble family from the common folk. The king and queen are currently bedridden and unconscious as a result of [[The Ruby+Silver Necklace]].
