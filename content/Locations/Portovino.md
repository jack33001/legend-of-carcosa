Nestled on the southern coast of the island of [[Sanstrelli]], [[Portovino]] is a place of stark contrasts. The town is picturesque, with its sunlit vineyards, cobblestone streets, and a bustling harbor. But beneath its charm lies an ever-present underworld dominated by [[L’ombra Profonda (The Tartuffo Family)]] (“The Deep Shadow), the Tartuffo Family mafia. This criminal organization has woven itself into the fabric of [[Portovino]], balancing on a razor’s edge between protector and predator.
