A partially submerged church, standing out of the water about a hundred feet off of the coast of [[Pillartown]]. A long pontoon gives a path for visitors. 

The salt-worn stone walls rise seventy feet out of the water; the holes where windows used to reside host a cool sea breeze. Knee deep water floods the inside of the church. Wet, rotting pews sit in the water, their bottoms crusted with barnacles. A large five-pointed star emitting strange, glowing rays is painted on the front of the church. The water level inside remains relatively constant, regardless of tide.

Near the back of the basilica, a dark tunnel descends vertically into the water. The tunnel reaches down about sixty feet, the bottom of which is a cavern lined with driftwood and the remains of old boats. The water is cold. 

In the main chamber of the basilica, a strange plant hangs from a chandelier. [[Hen]]had the insight that it's probably a map of the tunnels below.
