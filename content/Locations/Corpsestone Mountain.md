An enormous mountain the the center of [[The Maw]]. The mountain is actually the corpse of a long-dead warbeast of the gods, which came here to die after being slain by a city (likely [[Carcosa]]). [[Da Lo Lensky]] lives in his tower atop the mountain, siphoning life from the creature.
