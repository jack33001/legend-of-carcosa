The skeleton of a long-dead dragon turtle, that washed up on the shores of [[Pillartown]] long ago. Now, it's the main bar of the town.

The musky scent of alcohol and spiced wine permeates the place. It's partially submerged at the far end - everyone sits in the dry area, but when the tide is high it might still be ankle-deep water throughout. The bar is a single slab of bone, tended by a dragonborn with a wooden hand.
