
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

**Factions:** [[The Red Quarter Rebels]], [[The Ny'alkosh Family]], [[The Lords of Carcosa]]

------------------------------------------------------------------------

The rebellion of [[The Red Quarter Rebels]], in which [[The Leviathan]] was let loose on the city of [[Ny'al]] and the city was put under siege. Although it was between the city's two factions, [[The Lords of Carcosa]] are to blame for creating the tensions leading to the event.
