>Rhythmic flashes stun the crowding darkness for moments at a time. The noise of the impacts reverberates throughout the open cavern with nauseating intensity. Metal is molded and formed, stretched, and torn, unrelenting workmanship forces itself upon stubborn steel. Every rivet and sprocket, a cherished and beautiful craft contorted into the horrific whole. The darkness of night begins to wane painting a picture of men clambering along gargantuan machines, like ants crawling down a towering silhouette. The song strummed by hammers and spells, sweat and steel take on a new rhythm, a titanic heartbeat.
